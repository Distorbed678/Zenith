# Getting started

> Zenith is in its foundation phase. The engine is not playable yet. Follow progress on the
> [tracker](https://distorbed678.github.io/Zenith/tracker).

## For players (once releases exist)

1. Install the matching game version in your launcher and start it once.
2. Download Zenith for your platform from the [downloads page](https://distorbed678.github.io/Zenith/downloads).
3. Open the Zenith launcher. It finds your install, runs the ownership checks, and imports your instances.
